# LITS Trademark & Conformance-Mark Policy

Openness applies to the **specification**. The LITS contract is open (Apache-2.0 / CC-BY — see
[LICENSING.md](./LICENSING.md)). **"LITS" itself is the common, descriptive name** of a livestock
identification & traceability system and is **not** claimed as an exclusive trademark. What *is*
protected — to keep an open standard accountable and prevent impersonation — are the distinctive
**national instance names** and the **conformance mark**: anyone may implement the API, but only
accredited parties may present themselves as official.

## The marks

- The **national instance names** — e.g. **"ZLITS"** (Zimbabwe), **"MLITS"** (Malawi), and further
  per-country instances — which are coined, distinctive and registrable.
- The **conformance mark / badge** (e.g. a "LITS-conformant" badge).

"LITS" / "livestock identification & traceability system" is generic and used descriptively; it is
not exclusively claimed.

## Ownership

The marks are **held by the steward and transfer to the State / delegated operator on official
designation** (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) §2). They are licensed to others only as set
out below.

## Permitted use (no permission required)

- **Nominative / factual reference** — stating truthfully that your product *"integrates with the
  LITS API"* or is *"built to the LITS contract,"* in plain text, without logos.
- Referring to the open contract by name in documentation, articles and tenders.

## Use that requires accreditation

- Describing a product as **conformant**, or **displaying the conformance mark/badge**, requires
  that the product is an **accredited, conformance-tested integrator** holding a live per-operator
  API key (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) §5). Accreditation may be suspended or withdrawn;
  the right to use the mark ends with it.

## Prohibited use

- Presenting **any deployment, fork or copy** as **an official, national or accredited registry**
  for any jurisdiction. The official instance is the one designated by the State and run by the
  licensed operator.
- Using a protected name or the conformance mark so as to **imply State endorsement, accreditation
  or official status** you do not hold.
- Using a national instance name (ZLITS, MLITS, …), or a confusingly similar name, as your own
  product or company name, domain, or logo.
- Altering the marks, or combining them with your own so as to suggest a single origin.

## Why this matters

A spoofed "official certificate" or a fork posing as the national system would mislead keepers,
abattoirs and border officers and undermine the registry's whole value. The trademark — not the
(open) code — is the control that prevents impersonation. **Open spec + protected names +
accreditation = open to build on, impossible to pass off as official.**

## Enforcement & questions

The steward (and, after designation, the State / operator) may act to stop misleading or
unauthorised use. Questions about permitted use or accreditation: contact the steward / operator
named in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).
