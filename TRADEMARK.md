# ZLITS Trademark & Conformance-Mark Policy

Openness applies to the **specification**, never to the **name**. The ZLITS contract is
open (Apache-2.0 / CC-BY — see [LICENSING.md](./LICENSING.md)); the **"ZLITS" word-mark** and the
**ZLITS conformance mark** are *not* covered by those licences. This policy keeps an open standard
accountable: anyone may implement the API, but only accredited parties may present themselves as
official.

## The marks

- **"ZLITS"** and **"Zimbabwe Livestock Identification & Traceability System"** (the word-marks).
- The **ZLITS conformance mark / badge** (e.g. "ZLITS-conformant").

## Ownership

The marks are **held by the ZLITS steward and transfer to the State / delegated operator on
official designation** (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) §2). They are licensed to others only
as set out below.

## Permitted use (no permission required)

- **Nominative / factual reference** — stating truthfully that your product *"integrates with the
  ZLITS API"* or is *"built to the ZLITS contract,"* in plain text, without logos.
- Referring to the open contract by name in documentation, articles and tenders.

## Use that requires accreditation

- Describing a product as **"ZLITS-conformant,"** or **displaying the conformance mark/badge**,
  requires that the product is an **accredited, conformance-tested integrator** holding a live
  per-operator API key (see [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md) §5). Accreditation may be suspended or
  withdrawn; the right to use the mark ends with it.

## Prohibited use

- Presenting **any deployment, fork or copy** of the reference implementation as **an official,
  national or accredited ZLITS registry** for any jurisdiction. The official instance is the one
  designated by the State and run by the licensed operator — see `reference-impl/LICENSE`.
- Using the marks in a way that **implies State endorsement, accreditation or official status** you
  do not hold.
- Using the marks, or a confusingly similar name, as your own product or company name, domain, or
  logo.
- Altering the marks, or combining them with your own so as to suggest a single origin.

## Why this matters

A spoofed "official ZLITS certificate" or a fork posing as the national system would mislead
keepers, abattoirs and border officers and undermine the registry's whole value. The trademark —
not the (open) code — is the control that prevents impersonation. **Open spec + trademark +
accreditation = open to build on, impossible to fake.**

## Enforcement & questions

The steward (and, after designation, the State / operator) may act to stop misleading or
unauthorised use. Questions about permitted use or accreditation: contact the steward / operator
named in [GOVERNANCE.md](./GOVERNANCE.md).
